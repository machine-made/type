About this sketch

What it shows, and what it does not yet

  • Curves are superellipses with one exponent, 2.6, so rounds are squarish and the same everywhere. A real cut tunes each bowl.
  • Spacing is one sidebearing that grows with weight. There is no spacing or kerning pass yet.
  • Contrast is one ratio: horizontals are 84 percent of the stem. There are no ink traps and no optical sizes.
  • The ampersand, accents and most symbols are not drawn yet.
  • This page draws with canvas. The font itself will be written by the loom and compiled by fontmake, like Weft.
