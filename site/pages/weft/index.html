The FRAG axis

From clean to broken on one axis

FRAG runs from 0 to 100. Up to 15 it only cuts small notches into the joints, so body text stays safe. From 16 it cuts across the strokes. Past 50 the pieces move and drop out.

  1. 0CleanBody text
  2. 1–15Ink-trap notchesBody text
  3. 16–50Cuts across strokesHeadings
  4. 51–100Broken piecesTitles and marks

Proven readable

Read back by OCR at every FRAG level

CI sets a test text in Weft at each FRAG level, renders it and reads it back with OCR. The character error rate (CER) is the share of characters the OCR gets wrong. The release gate: up to FRAG 15, CER must match FRAG 0.

The loom

A Python generator. It reads a table of centre-line strokes, adds square caps and miter joins, removes overlaps and writes a UFO master at 1000 units per em. No outline comes from another font.
