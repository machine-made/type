Construction

Four parts and a level cut

No curve is drawn by hand. Rounds are superellipses with one exponent, 2.6. Diagonals keep their optical weight. Each opening is a level cut through one ring, which is where the Swiss terminals come from.

  • HStemA rectangle. Stems keep their weight at every width.
  • ORingA superellipse, n = 2.6. Inner and outer share the exponent.
  • VDiagonalA parallelogram or a V, widened so it reads as heavy as a stem.
  • CCutA level cut on one ring: the horizontal terminal.

Made by machines

From a sketch to a font

Agents write the generator, the tests and the CI. People set the goals and review the proofs.

The loom

A Python generator. It ports the approved sketch engine, builds every glyph from the four parts, and writes 16 UFO masters (weight × width) with the same points in each. fontmake compiles them. No outline comes from another font.

Read the loom

Proofs

The sketch engine and the font are rendered side by side at the corners of the designspace. A drift report measures every glyph between the masters.
