A reading sans based on Plus Jakarta Sans. Straight forms are the default, and the sharp forms are one stylistic set away, so it sits next to Weft.

machine-made Heddle

Grotesk 1958

A Swiss display grotesk for posters, headlines and big figures. Every letter is built from four parts and level cuts. A width axis runs from Compressed to Extended.

machine-made Warp

Drawn by a pen, for papers and maths.

A Modern serif with vertical stress. Every letter is a few centre-lines swept by one level pen. Full basic Greek, maths symbols, small capitals and old-style figures.
