machine-made Sans

A reading sans for text and interfaces. It keeps the geometry, tall x-height and open counters of Plus Jakarta Sans, and makes the sharp and straight alternates the default forms.

Every result a machine hands you is checked by another machine.

machine-made Sans sits next to Weft. Its x-height is tall and its counters are open, so it stays clear at small sizes on screens. The sharp and straight forms give it the same cut, flat look as the monospace.

Default forms

Sharp and straight by default

Plus Jakarta Sans ships three sets of alternates: sharp (ss01), straight (ss02) and swirl ({{SANS_SWIRL_TAG}}). machine-made Sans makes sharp and straight the default. Swirl stays available as a stylistic set.
