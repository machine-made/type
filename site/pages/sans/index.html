machine-made Sans

A reading sans for text and interfaces. It keeps the geometry, tall x-height and open counters of Plus Jakarta Sans, makes the straight alternates the default forms, and puts the sharp forms in stylistic set ss02.

Every result a machine hands you is checked by another machine.

machine-made Sans sits next to Weft. Its x-height is tall and its counters are open, so it stays clear at small sizes on screens. The straight forms give it a plain, flat look, and the sharp set in ss02 gives it the cut look of the monospace.

Default forms

Straight by default, sharp in ss02

Plus Jakarta Sans ships three sets of alternates: sharp, straight and swirl. machine-made Sans makes the straight a j p q r y the default. The sharp forms are in ss02, the original round forms in ss01, and swirl in {{SANS_SWIRL_TAG}}.
